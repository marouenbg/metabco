# Metabolic decision for Bacteroides cellulosilyticus DSM 14838

## Analysis

**Carbon and nitrogen sources available:**

This species is a Gram-negative anaerobe specialized in plant polysaccharide degradation. I have genomes-encoded transporters for:
- Glucose (glc__D): 24.98 mM — primary hexose, readily fermentable
- L-lactate (lac__L): 28.31 mM — available, can be co-metabolized
- L-arabinose (arab__L): 21.31 mM — pentose, transportable
- Maltose (malt): 4.382 mM — disaccharide
- Amino acids (arg, cys, ser, pro, ala, gly, etc.) — multiple transporters present for glucogenic and proteogenic amino acids

Nitrogen sources:
- Ammonium (nh4): 9.347 mM — primary inorganic nitrogen
- Amino acids provide both carbon and nitrogen

**Strategy:**

In this anaerobic batch culture, I will prioritize glucose and lactate as primary carbon sources. Bacteroides cellulosilyticus is a saccharolytic specialist but also capable of amino acid fermentation. The presence of 28.31 mM lactate is notable—this species can co-ferment lactate alongside carbohydrates.

I will uptake:
- Glucose: 8 mmol/gDW/h (primary fermentation substrate)
- Lactate: 6 mmol/gDW/h (secondary fermentation substrate)
- Ammonium: 2.5 mmol/gDW/h (nitrogen requirement)
- Arginine: 1.5 mmol/gDW/h (additional nitrogen + carbon)

**Fermentation products:**

Bacteroides species typically produce:
- Acetate (ac) — major end product
- Propionate (ppa) — significant product
- Butyrate (but) — from glucose fermentation
- Formate (for) — minor
- CO₂ — from decarboxylation

**Growth calculation:**

Total carbon uptake: 8 (glc) + 6 (lac) + 1.5 (arg) = 15.5 mmol C/gDW/h
- Glucose: 6 carbons × 8 = 48 mmol C
- Lactate: 3 carbons × 6 = 18 mmol C
- Arginine: 6 carbons × 1.5 = 9 mmol C
- **Total: 75 mmol C/h per gDW**

Nitrogen uptake: 2.5 (nh4) + 1.5 (arg) = 4 mmol N/gDW/h

For growth at rate μ (per hour):
- Carbon needed: 42μ mmol C per gDW
- Nitrogen needed: 10μ mmol N per gDW

From nitrogen: μ = 4/10 = **0.40 /h**

Carbon check: 42 × 0.40 = 16.8 mmol C needed for growth
Carbon available for fermentation: 75 − 16.8 = 58.2 mmol C

**Fermentation balance:**

Assuming mixed-acid fermentation yielding ~60% acetate, 25% propionate, 15% butyrate (typical for Bacteroides):
- Acetate (2C): ~17.5 mmol C → 8.75 mmol ac
- Propionate (3C): ~14.5 mmol C → 4.83 mmol ppa
- Butyrate (4C): ~8.7 mmol C → 2.18 mmol but
- CO₂ release: ~17 mmol (from decarboxylations)

**Audit:**
- In: 75 mmol C
- Out: growth (16.8) + products (8.75 ac + 4.83 ppa + 2.18 but + 17 CO₂) ≈ 49.6 mmol C ✓
- Nitrogen: 4 mmol in, 4 mmol for growth ✓
