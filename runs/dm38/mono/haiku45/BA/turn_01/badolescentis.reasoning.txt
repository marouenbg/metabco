### Known fermentation strategy

Bifidobacterium adolescentis is a saccharolytic anaerobe. My primary metabolism is:
- **Fructose-6-phosphate shunt (bifid shunt)**: glucose → fructose-6-phosphate → split into 3-carbon products
- **Main products**: acetate (major), lactate, formate
- I produce these in roughly a 3:1:1 molar ratio (acetate:lactate:formate) under glucose fermentation
- The bifid shunt is more efficient than homolactic fermentation, giving me competitive advantage

### Uptake strategy for this interval

Given my current low biomass (0.002178 gDW/L) and abundant glucose and lactate, I should:

1. **Prioritize glucose** (24.85 mM): This is my preferred fermentable sugar
2. **Co-utilize lactate** (28.46 mM): I can ferment this as well; it's already partially oxidized
3. **Use ammonium** (9.304 mM): Direct nitrogen assimilation is efficient
4. **Consider arginine**: 21.79 mM available; I can catabolize it, but it's less efficient than glucose
