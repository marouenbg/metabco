# Metabolic Decision: Bifidobacterium adolescentis ATCC 15703, Turn 0

## Analysis

### Carbon and Nitrogen Sources Available

**Primary carbon sources I can metabolize:**
- D-glucose (24.98 mM): Bifidobacterium has excellent glucose utilization via the fructose-6-phosphate shunt and can ferment it to acetate and lactate
- L-lactate (28.31 mM): I can consume lactate as both carbon and energy source
- L-arabinose (21.31 mM): Pentose fermentation pathway available
- Maltose (4.382 mM): Disaccharide utilization
- myo-inositol (6.272 mM): Fermentable via inositol dehydrogenase

**Nitrogen sources:**
- Ammonium (9.347 mM): Primary nitrogen source, readily assimilated
- L-arginine (21.81 mM): Can be catabolized for both C and N
- L-cysteine (8.4 mM): Contains both C and N; can be used
- Other amino acids present in modest amounts

**Why I cannot use some present compounds:**
- CO₂ (47.62 mM): I am a heterotroph; cannot fix CO₂ autotrophically
- Nitrate (0.1093 mM): I lack dissimilatory nitrate reductase; cannot use as electron acceptor in anaerobic conditions
- Trace vitamins/cofactors: These are growth factors, not primary carbon sources

### Metabolic Strategy

**Substrate hierarchy:** I will prioritize glucose (fast, efficient fermentation) and use arginine as a secondary nitrogen source while ammonium is available. This maximizes growth rate in early stationary phase.

**Fermentation products:** Bifidobacterium adolescentis is a bifid fermenter, producing primarily:
- Acetate (major)
- Lactate (major)
- Formate (minor)
- Trace ethanol

The bifid pathway converts glucose to acetyl-CoA and pyruvate, yielding ~2.5 mol acetate + 1.5 mol lactate per mol glucose.

### Growth Calculation

**Glucose uptake strategy:** Start with 15 mmol/gDW/h glucose uptake (realistic for batch culture with high substrate availability)

From 1 glucose (6C):
- Bifid fermentation yields ~2.5 acetate (5C) + 1.5 lactate (3C) = 8C total (reasonable with CO₂ loss ~1C)
- Actual: ~2.4 acetate + 1.6 lactate per glucose

**Carbon balance for growth:**
- Glucose uptake: 15 mmol/gDW/h = 90 mmol C/gDW/h
- Growth requirement: 33 mmol C/gDW/h
- Available for fermentation products: 57 mmol C/gDW/h

**Nitrogen balance:**
- Growth requirement: 8 mmol N/gDW/h
- Ammonium uptake: 5 mmol/gDW/h (conservative, preserving pool)
- Arginine uptake: 3 mmol/gDW/h (provides 3 mmol N/gDW/h)
- Total: 8 mmol N/gDW/h ✓

**Growth rate derivation:**
- With 15 mmol glucose/gDW/h and ~6 mmol N/gDW/h available
- Nitrogen is the limiting factor at 8 mmol N/gDW/h needed per gram biomass
- Growth rate = 8 mmol N available / 8 mmol N required = **1.0 /h**

This is realistic for early exponential phase on rich medium.

### Fermentation Products

From 15 mmol glucose + 3 mmol arginine (9C from Arg):
- Total C in: 90 + 27 = 117 mmol C/gDW/h
- C for growth: 33 mmol C/gDW/h
- C for fermentation: 84 mmol C/gDW/h
- Acetate production: ~50 mmol/gDW/h
- Lactate production: ~30 mmol/gDW/h
- Formate: ~4 mmol/gDW/h

**Audit:**
- C in: 117 mmol
- C out (products + growth): 50 + 30 + 4 + 33 = 117 mmol ✓
- N in: 5 + 3 = 8 mmol
- N out (growth): 8 mmol ✓
