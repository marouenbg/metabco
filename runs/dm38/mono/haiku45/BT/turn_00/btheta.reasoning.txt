1. **Prioritize glucose** — it is abundant (24.98 mM), energy-rich, and my preferred fermentable substrate
2. **Use ammonium as primary nitrogen** — it is the most readily assimilated (9.347 mM available)
3. **Ferment glucose to short-chain fatty acids** — as a Bacteroides species in anaerobic conditions, I produce acetate, propionate, and butyrate as primary fermentation products
4. **Maintain redox balance** — in anaerobic fermentation, I must balance electron flow; typical products reflect this
