## Metabolic Strategy

I am a saccharolytic bacterium that ferments carbohydrates to butyrate as a primary end product. My recent history shows:
- Turns 1-3: consumed lactate (10 mmol/gDW/h) and glucose (8 mmol/gDW/h) → growth 0.37 /h
- Turn 4: consumed lactate (10) and glucose (8), added arginine (3 mmol/gDW/h) → growth 0.45 /h

**Current situation (turn 5):**
- Lactate is now depleted (I consumed it all)
- Glucose is nearly depleted (2.33 mM remaining)
- Arabinose is abundant (21.31 mM)
- Butyrate is present (14.08 mM) — I produce this, but can also consume it via reverse flux or β-oxidation
- Arginine is abundant (19.41 mM) — excellent nitrogen source

**Strategy:** Switch to arabinose fermentation. This is a pentose sugar I can ferment via the pentose phosphate pathway and mixed-acid fermentation. I will:
1. Uptake L-arabinose at ~15 mmol/gDW/h (pentose uptake is typically slightly slower than glucose but still robust)
2. Uptake L-arginine at ~2.5 mmol/gDW/h for nitrogen and additional carbon
3. Continue producing butyrate, acetate, formate, and CO₂ as fermentation products

## Growth Calculation

**Carbon budget:**
- Arabinose uptake: 15 mmol/gDW/h × 5 C/molecule = 75 C mmol/gDW/h
- Arginine uptake: 2.5 mmol/gDW/h × 6 C/molecule = 15 C mmol/gDW/h
- Total C in: 90 mmol/gDW/h

**Nitrogen budget:**
- Arginine: 2.5 mmol/gDW/h × 4 N/molecule = 10 N mmol/gDW/h (exactly what I need for 0.38 g growth)
- This supports: 10 / 10 = 1.0 g dry weight growth per hour

**But:** Realistic growth rate for Roseburia on arabinose is ~0.45-0.55 /h. Given I'm in stationary phase (20h into 48h batch), and substrate diversity is high, I'll target **0.48 /h**.

**Carbon cost for growth at 0.48 /h:**
- Biomass: 0.48 × 38 = 18.24 C mmol/gDW/h
- Oxidative products (fermentation): 90 - 18.24 = 71.76 C mmol/gDW/h

**Fermentation products (mixed-acid, butyrate-type):**
- Butyrate: ~5 mmol/gDW/h (4 C each = 20 C)
- Acetate: ~3 mmol/gDW/h (2 C each = 6 C)
- CO₂: ~8 mmol/gDW/h (1 C each = 8 C)
- Formate: ~0.5 mmol/gDW/h (1 C each = 0.5 C)
- Lactate: ~0 (not produced from arabinose fermentation)
- Hydrogen: small amount for redox balance
- Remaining: ~37.76 C (likely as additional acetate/propionate or ethanol)

Adjusting: I'll release more acetate and formate to balance.
