## Fermentation strategy

Given anaerobic conditions and abundant fermentable substrates, my strategy:
1. **Primary substrate: glucose** — fastest growth, well-characterized Embden-Meyerhof fermentation
2. **Secondary substrate: lactate** — also readily fermented
3. **Products**: acetate (main), formate, H₂, CO₂ (typical Blautia fermentation signature)

Glucose fermentation stoichiometry (simplified):
- C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2 C₂H₅COOH (ethanol) or → 3 CH₃COOH (acetate) or mixed
- Mixed fermentation typically yields ~2 mol acetate + formate/H₂ per glucose
