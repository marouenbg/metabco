## Growth constraint calculation

- **Nitrogen**: ~7 mmol N per gram DW

- Nitrogen needed: 1.2 × 7 = 8.4 mmol N/gDW

- Over 4 hours: 28 mmol C available for growth + catabolism

- Growth rate: **μ = 0.25 /h** is sustainable

- I will take up remaining butyrate and acetate
- I will release **acetate**, **formate**, and **CO₂** as overflow products
- I will scavenge amino acids to supplement nitrogen
