For growth, I need:
- ~33 mmol C per gDW
- ~7 mmol N per gDW

This supports only 0.363 / 7 = **0.052 gDW of new biomass maximum**.

Over 4 hours: maximum growth rate = 0.052 / 4 / 4.466 = **0.003 /h**

This is negligible. I should enter **stationary phase with minimal growth**.
