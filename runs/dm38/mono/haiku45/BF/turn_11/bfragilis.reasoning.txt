# Metabolic reasoning for Bacteroides fragilis at turn 11 (t=44h)

## Assessment of the situation

I am at 3.53 gDW/L, well into stationary phase of a 48-hour batch culture. Nutrient depletion is advancing. I must evaluate what I can actually metabolise.

## Carbon and nitrogen sources I can transport

**Primary carbon sources available and usable:**
- **Butyrate (21.05 mM)**: I can ferment this via β-oxidation to acetyl-CoA → acetate and CO₂. This is a core Bacteroides fragilis pathway.
- **Acetate (18.4 mM)**: I can activate to acetyl-CoA and use for biosynthesis and energy.
- **Formate (19.27 mM)**: I can oxidise this to CO₂ and H₂, or use in one-carbon metabolism.
- **Succinate (0.626 mM)**: Usable via TCA cycle fragments in anaerobic conditions.
- **Amino acids (tyrosine, lysine, phenylalanine, etc.)**: I can catabolise these via the Stickland reaction and amino acid fermentation pathways. These provide both carbon and nitrogen.

**Sources I cannot use:**
- **Nitrate (0.1093 mM)**: Bacteroides fragilis is strictly anaerobic and lacks nitrate reductase in this strain.
- **Orotate, nucleobases**: These are too specialized for primary energy generation.

**Nitrogen sources:**
- **Ammonium (1.496 mM)**: Limited but directly assimilable.
- **Amino acids themselves**: Provide both C and N through catabolism.

## Recent metabolic pattern

Turns 8–10 show I was growing on **mixed fermentation**:
- Consuming acetate and butyrate
- Producing butyrate, acetate, formate, and CO₂
- This is consistent with amino acid fermentation (Stickland-like) and short-chain fatty acid metabolism

Turn 10 showed a major shift: I consumed 8 mmol/gDW/h of butyrate (a huge uptake), suggesting I switched to butyrate fermentation as a primary energy source. Growth dropped to 0.094 /h.

## Current strategy (turn 11)

At t=44h with only 4h remaining, I am resource-limited. Butyrate and acetate are still relatively abundant. Amino acids are depleted. Ammonium is severely limiting at 1.496 mM.

**Fermentation products I make:**
- Acetate (major end product)
- Butyrate (from acetyl-CoA condensation)
- Formate (from pyruvate)
- CO₂ (from decarboxylation)
- Propionate (minor, from odd-chain fermentation)

## Growth rate calculation

With **ammonium at 1.496 mM** and current biomass at 3.53 gDW/L:
- I need ~8 mmol N per gram of new biomass
- Maximum N available: 1.496 mmol/L ÷ 3.53 gDW/L = **0.424 mmol N per gDW**
- This supports: 0.424 ÷ 8 = **0.053 gDW per gDW**, or about **0.053 /h** maximum growth if I can generate sufficient carbon

**Carbon budget:**
- Butyrate (21.05 mM) at 4 carbons/molecule = 84.2 mmol C/L ÷ 3.53 gDW/L = **23.9 mmol C/gDW**
- Acetate (18.4 mM) at 2 carbons/molecule = 36.8 mmol C/L ÷ 3.53 gDW/L = **10.4 mmol C/gDW**
- Formate (19.27 mM) at 1 carbon/molecule = 19.27 mmol C/L ÷ 3.53 gDW/L = **5.5 mmol C/gDW**
- **Total: ~40 mmol C/gDW available**

To support growth at **0.05 /h**:
- Biosynthesis cost: 0.05 × 41 = **2.05 mmol C/gDW** (consumed)
- Fermentation overhead (assume 30% of carbon is respired as CO₂): 0.30 × uptake
- If I take up ~12 mmol C/gDW for growth + maintenance: ~3.6 mmol CO₂ released

**Realistic uptake strategy:**
- Butyrate: -8.0 mmol/gDW/h (primary fermentation substrate)
- Acetate: -3.0 mmol/gDW/h (biosynthesis and energy)
- Formate: -2.0 mmol/gDW/h (supplemental one-carbon)
- Ammonium: -0.4 mmol/gDW/h (limited by availability)

**Products:**
- Acetate: +2.5 mmol/gDW/h (fermentation end product)
- CO₂: +2.8 mmol/gDW/h (from decarboxylation)
- Butyrate: +1.0 mmol/gDW/h (from acetyl-CoA condensation)

**Carbon audit:**
- IN: (8 × 4) + (3 × 2) + (2 × 1) = 32 + 6 + 2 = **40 mmol C/gDW**
- OUT (growth): 0.048 × 41 = **1.97 mmol C/gDW**
- OUT (fermentation): (2.5 × 2) + (2.8 × 1) + (1.0 × 4) = 5 + 2.8 + 4 = **11.8 mmol C/gDW**
- Unaccounted: 40 − 1.97 − 11.8 = **26.2 mmol C/gDW** (this is respired as additional CO₂ or stored)

Adjusting: I should release more CO₂ to balance. Total CO₂ should be ~26 mmol/gDW/h to close the audit.

**Revised CO₂: +6.5 mmol/gDW/h**

**Growth rate:** With N-limited uptake of 0.4 mmol/gDW/h and C-sufficient, I estimate **0.048 /h** growth.
