## Metabolic strategy

As a saccharolytic and proteolytic anaerobe in a sealed batch culture, my strategy is:

1. **Prioritize glucose and arabinose** — these are abundant simple sugars that support rapid fermentation
2. **Use arginine** — abundant (21.81 mM) and provides both carbon and nitrogen; I have the arginine deiminase pathway
3. **Fermentation products** — as an obligate anaerobe, I ferment carbohydrates to acetate, propionate, butyrate, formate, and succinate. Given my metabolic capabilities, I'll produce primarily **acetate, butyrate, and formate** from glucose fermentation
4. **Nitrogen balance** — ammonium + arginine will support growth
