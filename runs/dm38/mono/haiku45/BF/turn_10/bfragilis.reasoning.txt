## Strategy at turn 10

I've already consumed most easily-fermentable carbohydrates (they were depleted earlier). Now I'm in the **late stationary phase** of a 48-hour batch culture, at 40 hours in.

**Remaining strategy:**
1. Continue catabolizing the abundant short-chain fatty acids (acetate, butyrate, formate) — these are products of earlier fermentation that I can re-oxidize or use for gluconeogenesis and biosynthesis
2. Use amino acids selectively for nitrogen and carbon, not wastefully
3. Reduce growth rate as nutrient availability declines

**Growth calculation:**
- Butyrate (31.93 mM) is the most abundant usable carbon source
- At modest uptake of butyrate (~8 mmol/gDW/h) + acetate (~4 mmol/gDW/h) + formate (~3 mmol/gDW/h), I can acquire ~15 mmol C/gDW/h
- Growth requires ~41 mmol C/gDW/h, so at 15 mmol C/gDW/h input, I can support growth of roughly: 15/41 ≈ **0.37 /h** if nitrogen is not limiting
- Nitrogen: I'll take up glycine, cysteine, and other amino acids (~3–4 mmol N/gDW/h total from mixed uptake). This is ~8 mmol N/gDW/h requirement ÷ 4 uptake ≈ **0.18 /h** nitrogen-limited
- **Realistic growth rate: ~0.18–0.22 /h**, limited by nitrogen availability
