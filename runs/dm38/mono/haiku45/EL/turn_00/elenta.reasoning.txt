# Metabolic Decision for Eggerthella lenta DSM 2243

## Analysis

**Available carbon and nitrogen sources:**

Eggerthella lenta is a gram-positive anaerobe known to ferment carbohydrates and amino acids. Looking at what I can realistically transport:

- **Carbohydrates**: D-glucose (24.98 mM) and L-arabinose (21.31 mM) are primary fermentable sugars. I have PTS and other carbohydrate transporters.
- **Lactate**: L-lactate (28.31 mM) is abundant and I can both ferment and oxidize it anaerobically.
- **Amino acids**: L-arginine (21.81 mM), L-cysteine (8.4 mM), L-serine (6.4 mM), and others are present. I can transport and metabolize these.
- **Nitrogen**: Ammonium (9.347 mM) is the primary inorganic nitrogen source; arginine and other amino acids also provide nitrogen.

**Why not certain sources:**
- Nitrate (0.11 mM) is present but extremely limited and I lack strong nitrate reduction capacity.
- Trace vitamins and cofactors are present but not limiting.

**Fermentation strategy:**

Eggerthella lenta typically ferments glucose and other carbohydrates to lactate, acetate, and formate under anaerobic conditions. With lactate already present, I can perform mixed fermentation. The redox potential (-200 mV) supports anaerobic fermentation.

My strategy:
1. **Uptake glucose** (abundant, easy to ferment): ~8 mmol/gDW/h
2. **Uptake lactate** (abundant, can be fermented further): ~6 mmol/gDW/h
3. **Uptake arginine** (good nitrogen source): ~4 mmol/gDW/h
4. **Uptake ammonium** (primary N source): ~2 mmol/gDW/h
5. **Uptake cysteine** (sulfur + N + C): ~1 mmol/gDW/h

**Carbon and energy balance:**

- Glucose (C₆): 8 mmol/gDW/h = 48 mmol C/gDW/h
- Lactate (C₃): 6 mmol/gDW/h = 18 mmol C/gDW/h
- Arginine (C₆): 4 mmol/gDW/h = 24 mmol C/gDW/h
- Cysteine (C₃): 1 mmol/gDW/h = 3 mmol C/gDW/h
- **Total carbon in: 93 mmol C/gDW/h**

**Growth calculation:**

Growth requires ~33 mmol C/gDW and ~8 mmol N/gDW per gram dry weight produced.

Nitrogen available:
- Arginine: 4 mmol/gDW/h × 4 N atoms = 16 mmol N/gDW/h
- Ammonium: 2 mmol/gDW/h = 2 mmol N/gDW/h
- Cysteine: 1 mmol/gDW/h × 1 N = 1 mmol N/gDW/h
- **Total N in: ~19 mmol N/gDW/h**

Growth rate limited by N: 19 / 8 = **2.375 /h** (unrealistic)
Growth rate limited by C: 93 / 33 = **2.82 /h** (unrealistic)

These are maximum theoretical rates. Realistic anaerobic growth for Eggerthella is 0.3–0.6 /h. I'll use **0.45 /h**.

**Carbon audit at µ = 0.45 /h:**
- Carbon in: 93 mmol/gDW/h
- Carbon for growth: 0.45 × 33 = 14.85 mmol C/gDW/h
- Carbon for fermentation products: 93 − 14.85 = 78.15 mmol C/gDW/h

**Fermentation products:**

Typical Eggerthella fermentation of glucose + lactate yields acetate, formate, and some lactate recycling:
- Acetate (C₂): ~30 mmol/gDW/h
- Formate (C₁): ~25 mmol/gDW/h
- CO₂ (from decarboxylation): ~15 mmol/gDW/h
- Remaining as lactate or other: ~8 mmol/gDW/h

This accounts for ~78 mmol C/gDW/h ✓

Nitrogen balance: 19 mmol N in − (0.45 × 8) = 15.4 mmol N excess → release as NH₄ or urea.
