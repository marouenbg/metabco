# Metabolic Decision for Bacteroides ovatus — Turn 9

## Analysis

**Carbon and nitrogen sources available:**

I am a Gram-negative anaerobic bacterium with a well-characterized saccharolytic metabolism. My genome encodes:
- Carbohydrate PTS systems and permeases (maltose, arabinose historically used)
- Amino acid transporters (broad specificity for branched-chain and aromatic amino acids)
- Organic acid uptake (acetate, propionate, lactate, succinate, formate)
- Limited capacity for complex polysaccharide degradation in this minimal medium

**Current nutrient status (turn 9, t=36h, 48h total):**

We are 75% through the batch culture. Preferred carbohydrates (maltose 4.4 mM) are nearly exhausted. Amino acids remain abundant (glycine, serine, leucine, valine, phenylalanine, tyrosine all >2.5 mM). Acetate and propionate are present but limited (21.5 and 18 mM respectively). Nitrogen is scarce: no ammonium detected, only amino acids and trace nitrate (0.11 mM).

**Growth strategy:**

At this late stage with limited time remaining (12 h), I should:
1. Continue consuming available amino acids as both carbon and nitrogen sources
2. Ferment amino acids via the Stickland reaction (paired oxidation-reduction of amino acids)
3. Produce short-chain fatty acids (acetate, propionate, butyrate) as primary fermentation products
4. Maintain modest growth rate while conserving remaining resources

**Amino acid catabolism:**

I will take up glycine, serine, leucine, valine, phenylalanine, and tyrosine. These support both carbon and nitrogen needs. Typical Bacteroides fermentation of amino acids yields:
- Acetate and propionate (major products)
- CO₂, H₂ (minor)
- Ammonia (from deamination)

**Stoichiometry:**

Taking up ~8 mmol amino acids/gDW/h (mix of small and large amino acids, averaging ~4 carbons and ~1 nitrogen per molecule):
- Carbon in: ~32 mmol C/gDW/h
- Nitrogen in: ~8 mmol N/gDW/h
- Growth cost: 42 mmol C + 10 mmol N per gDW produced
- Feasible growth: limited by nitrogen; 8 mmol N supports ~0.8 gDW growth, but carbon allows only 32/42 ≈ 0.76 gDW

**Realistic growth rate: 0.35–0.40 /h** (supported by amino acid fermentation)
