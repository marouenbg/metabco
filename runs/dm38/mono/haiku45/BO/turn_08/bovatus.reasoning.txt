## Growth Rate Calculation

**Carbon budget:**
- Acetate uptake: 12 mmol/gDW/h (moderate rate; not maximal to avoid overflow)
- Amino acid uptake (mainly proline + arginine): 3 mmol C/gDW/h equivalent
- Total C in: ~15 mmol/gDW/h

**Carbon costs:**
- Growth at rate μ requires: 42 × μ mmol C/gDW
- Fermentation (typical Y_x/s for Bacteroides ~0.15): if consuming 15 mmol C, ~2.25 gDW produced, requiring ~95 mmol C total
- This is unsustainable. Realistic: lower uptake or lower growth.

**Revised estimate:**
- Acetate: 8 mmol/gDW/h
- Proline: 2 mmol/gDW/h (nitrogen + carbon)
- Arginine: 1 mmol/gDW/h (nitrogen + carbon)
- Total C in: ~11 mmol/gDW/h
- Nitrogen in: ~1.5 mmol N/gDW/h (from amino acids + NH₄: 1.5 mmol/gDW/h)
- Growth rate: 11 / (42 + 10×1.5/1.5) ≈ 0.18 /h (nitrogen slightly limiting but acceptable)

**Carbon audit:**
- In: 11 mmol C/gDW/h
- Biomass: 0.18 gDW/h × 42 mmol C/gDW = 7.56 mmol C
- Fermentation products (out): 11 - 7.56 = 3.44 mmol C/gDW/h as acetate/propionate/formate/CO₂

This is feasible. I'll release primarily acetate, propionate, CO₂, and minor formate.
