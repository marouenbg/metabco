## Growth calculation

**Strategy: Ferment maltose + amino acids**

Maltose (C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁) provides 12 carbons per molecule.
- Uptake: 4.38 mM maltose × 12 C/molecule = 52.56 mmol C available from maltose
- Plus amino acids: conservatively ~25 mmol C available from amino acids at modest uptake

Total available C: ~77.6 mmol C in the vessel for the next 4 h at my current density (2.748 gDW/L in 1 mL = 0.002748 gDW total)

For growth:
- Biomass production requires 38 mmol C per gDW
- I have 2.748 gDW already; if I grow at rate μ for 4 h, I need: 0.002748 × e^(μ×4) ≈ 0.002748 × (1 + 4μ) for small μ
- New biomass ≈ 0.002748 × 4μ = 0.010992μ gDW
- Carbon cost: 0.010992μ × 38 = 0.4176μ mmol C

**Realistic uptake rates:**
- Maltose: ~5 mmol/gDW/h (modest, as it's depleting)
- Amino acids (mix): ~4 mmol/gDW/h total
- Ammonium: ~0.5 mmol/gDW/h

At 2.748 gDW:
- Maltose uptake: 5 × 2.748 = 13.74 mmol/h
- Amino acid uptake (C): 4 × 2.748 = 10.99 mmol C/h
- Total C in: 24.73 mmol/h × 4 h = 98.92 mmol C available

**Growth rate calculation:**
- Growth requires ~38 C per gDW produced
- Fermentation products (acetate, formate, butyrate, propionate) account for ~60% of carbon; ~40% incorporated into biomass
- If 40% of 98.92 = 39.57 mmol C → biomass
- Biomass produced: 39.57 / 38 ≈ 1.04 gDW
- Growth rate: ln(1.04 / 2.748) / 4 ≈ -0.24 /h (negative, declining phase)

This is consistent with late stationary phase. I should lower expectations: **μ ≈ 0.05 /h** (slow maintenance metabolism)
