# Metabolic decision for Clostridium hiranonis TO-931

## Analysis

**What I can transport and what I cannot:**

C. hiranonis is a Gram-positive anaerobe known to ferment carbohydrates and amino acids. Looking at the abundant substrates:
- **D-glucose (24.86 mM)**: I have PTS or general hexose transporters. Readily available, excellent carbon source.
- **L-lactate (28.15 mM)**: I can ferment lactate via lactate dehydrogenase. Major substrate.
- **L-arginine (21.73 mM)**: I possess arginine deiminase pathway (ADI), a key feature of Clostridium species. Excellent nitrogen and carbon source.
- **L-arabinose (21.31 mM)**: Pentose fermentation is feasible but less efficient than glucose.
- **L-serine (6.4 mM)**, **L-cysteine (8.4 mM)**: Amino acids I can deaminate and ferment.
- **Ammonium (9.307 mM)**: Direct nitrogen source, always available.

**Known fermentation products:**
C. hiranonis ferments via mixed-acid fermentation, producing:
- **Butyrate** (primary product from glucose/lactate fermentation)
- **Acetate** (secondary product)
- **Formate** (minor)
- **H₂** (from formate lyase or direct hydrogenase)
- **CO₂** (from pyruvate decarboxylation)

The turn 0 data confirms this pattern: glucose and lactate uptake yielding acetate, butyrate, formate, H₂, and CO₂.

**Substrate choice and stoichiometry:**

At turn 0 (t=0), I grew at 0.403 /h. Now at t=4h with 0.0109 gDW/L, I should continue aggressive fermentation while abundant substrate remains.

My primary strategy: co-ferment **glucose + lactate + arginine**.

**Glucose fermentation (C₆H₁₂O₆ → products):**
- Glucose (6 C) → typically 2 acetyl-CoA + 2 pyruvate
- Under anaerobic conditions: pyruvate → acetate + formate + H₂, or → butyrate
- Net: ~3 mol acetate + 1 mol butyrate per glucose (rough estimate for Clostridium)

**Lactate fermentation (C₃H₆O₃ → products):**
- Lactate (3 C) → pyruvate → acetate + formate + H₂, or mixed products
- ~1.5 mol acetate + 0.5 mol butyrate per lactate

**Arginine fermentation via ADI (C₆H₁₄N₄O₂):**
- Arginine → citrulline + NH₃ (ADI)
- Citrulline → ornithine + CO₂ (carbamate kinase)
- Ornithine further catabolized
- Net: yields ATP, NH₃ (nitrogen), and carbon skeleton for fermentation
- ~2-3 mol C products per arginine
