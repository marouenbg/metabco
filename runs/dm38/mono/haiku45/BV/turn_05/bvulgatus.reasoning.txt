# Metabolic Decision: Bacteroides vulgatus ATCC 8482, Turn 5

## Analysis

**Situation assessment:**
- I am at 2.388 gDW/L after 20 hours, having grown well early then stalled
- The vessel is sealed; all resources are finite and accumulating
- I have already consumed the preferred sugars (glucose, arabinose, lactate)
- Turns 2–4 show zero growth, suggesting I hit a limitation

**Carbon and nitrogen sources I can transport:**
As a Bacteroides species, I have:
- Amino acid transporters (arg, cys, ala, gly, ser, leu, phe, tyr, pro, val, gln, asn, lys, thr, ile, his, met, trp, glu, asp all present)
- Short-chain fatty acid uptake (acetate, butyrate, propionate already being produced/consumed)
- Carbohydrate transporters (maltose is present at 4.4 mM, but I appear to have limited capacity)
- Inositol transport (6.3 mM available)

**Sources I cannot use effectively:**
- CO₂ is abundant (74 mM) but I cannot fix it autotrophically; it is a fermentation product
- Nitrate (0.11 mM) is too low and anaerobic nitrate reduction is not a Bacteroides strength

**The stall (turns 2–4):**
My recent fluxes show I was fermenting amino acids (consuming arg, nh4) and releasing acetate, butyrate, propionate, CO₂. But growth dropped to zero. This indicates **nitrogen limitation**. I consumed ammonium rapidly in early turns; the remaining free amino acids are now my only nitrogen source, but they must also provide carbon for growth.

**Nitrogen budget:**
- Biomass requires ~8 mmol N per gram DW produced
- Available free amino acids (most abundant): arg (8.8), cys (8.4), ala (5.3), gly (4.7)
- Total harvestable amino-acid nitrogen: ~60–70 mmol/L
- At 2.4 gDW/L, I have consumed ~19 mmol N already; ~50 mmol remains

**Strategy for next 4 hours:**
I will resume amino acid fermentation, focusing on the most abundant amino acids (arginine, cysteine, alanine) which provide both carbon and nitrogen. I will couple this with uptake of myo-inositol (6.3 mM, a polyol carbon source). This will support modest growth.

**Growth rate calculation:**
- If I consume 5 mmol/gDW/h of mixed amino acids (C₃–C₆, averaging ~4 carbons each = 20 mmol C/gDW/h) plus 2 mmol/gDW/h inositol (6 carbons = 12 mmol C/gDW/h)
- Total C uptake: ~32 mmol C/gDW/h
- Growth cost: 41 mmol C/gDW/h of growth
- Available for fermentation: 32 − (41 × μ) mmol C/gDW/h
- Nitrogen uptake: ~3 mmol N/gDW/h from amino acids
- Growth cost: 8 mmol N/gDW/h of growth → μ_max = 0.375 /h
- Carbon check: 32 mmol C in, minus 41 × 0.35 = 14.4 mmol C for growth = 17.6 mmol C for fermentation products ✓

I will target **μ = 0.35 /h**, a modest recovery from zero.

**Fermentation products:**
From amino acid deamination and mixed fermentation under anaerobic conditions, I will release acetate, butyrate, propionate, and CO₂ (and some H₂ if formate pathway is active).

**Carbon audit:**
- Uptake: arg (5.0) × 6C = 30; cys (2.0) × 3C = 6; ala (2.0) × 3C = 6; inositol (2.0) × 6C = 12 → **54 mmol C in**
- Growth: 0.35 μ × 41 = 14.4 mmol C
- Fermentation: 54 − 14.4 = 39.6 mmol C → release as ac, but, ppa, co₂ ✓
