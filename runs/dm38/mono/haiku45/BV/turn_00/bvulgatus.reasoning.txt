**Nitrogen sources:**

### Fermentation strategy

As an obligate anaerobe, I ferment sugars and amino acids to short-chain fatty acids (acetate, propionate, butyrate) and H₂, CO₂. For this early phase with abundant glucose and lactate, my primary strategy:

1. **Glucose fermentation** → acetate + butyrate + CO₂ + H₂
2. **Lactate co-fermentation** → acetate + propionate + butyrate
3. **Arginine fermentation** (if N becomes limiting) → citrulline → ornithine + CO₂ + NH₃

- C₆H₁₂O₆ → products; typical yield ~0.4 g biomass / g glucose
