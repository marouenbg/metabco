## Fermentation strategy

- **Lactate** (from pyruvate via LDH, especially when reducing power is abundant)
- **Acetate** (from acetyl-CoA, via acetate kinase)
- **Propionate** (from succinate via methylmalonyl-CoA pathway, or from alanine)
- **Formate** (from pyruvate formate-lyase under anaerobic stress)

Given anaerobic conditions and declining electron acceptors, I will favor:
1. **Acetate uptake** (21.35 mM available): Efficient; yields ATP via acetyl-CoA → oxaloacetate → succinate → propionate, or direct acetylation.
2. **Amino acid catabolism** (abundant): Proline, serine, alanine, glycine are good nitrogen sources and yield pyruvate or TCA intermediates.
3. **Formate uptake** (15.77 mM): Acts as both carbon and reducing power; formate oxidation regenerates NAD⁺.
4. **Propionate release**: Likely product from succinate reduction and amino acid catabolism.
5. **Lactate release**: Overflow metabolism if pyruvate accumulates.
