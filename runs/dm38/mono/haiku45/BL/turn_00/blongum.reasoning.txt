**What I cannot use effectively:**

- Nitrate (no3): 0.1093 mM — very low; I lack robust nitrate reductase for significant flux

## Fermentation Strategy

As *Bifidobacterium longum*, I am a facultative anaerobe specialized in bifidogenic fermentation:
- Primary pathway: fructose-6-phosphate shunt (hexose → acetate + lactate in roughly 1.5:2.5 ratio)
- I ferment glucose and other hexoses to **acetate** and **lactate** primarily
- With pentoses like arabinose, I produce similar products
- Arginine catabolism yields additional ammonia and organic acids

## Substrate Choice & Uptake Strategy

I will prioritize:
1. **D-glucose** (24.98 mM): fastest growth, most efficient
2. **L-arginine** (21.81 mM): dual C+N source, reduces need for separate N uptake
3. **L-arabinose** (21.31 mM): secondary pentose, also fermentable
4. **Ammonium** (9.347 mM): for amino acid synthesis

I will *not* heavily consume lactate at t=0 because I am growing on fresh glucose/arginine; lactate uptake typically occurs in stationary/decline phase.
