## Fermentation strategy

B. longum is a lactic acid bacterium but also produces:
- **Acetate** (from pyruvate via acetyl-CoA)
- **Lactate** (both D and L forms, though I preferentially make L-lactate)
- Minor: formate, succinate (via mixed-acid fermentation under stress)

Since I'm anaerobic and lactate is now abundant, I should:
1. Switch primary carbon to **lactate** (31.72 mM available)
2. Continue **arginine** uptake (12.9 mM, excellent N + C source)
3. Use remaining **glucose** (3.652 mM) opportunistically
4. Use **arabinose** (10.28 mM) as supplementary

## Growth rate calculation

- Lactate → pyruvate → acetyl-CoA + CO₂

- 6 carbon, 4 nitrogen per molecule
- Can be catabolized via arginine deiminase pathway → citrulline → ornithine
- Yields energy + carbon skeletons + ammonia
