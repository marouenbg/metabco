## 2. Fermentation products

D. longicatena is known to produce:
- **Butyrate** (primary product from carbohydrate fermentation via acetyl-CoA → butyrate)
- **Acetate** (from pyruvate, also via mixed-acid fermentation)
- **Formate** (from pyruvate decarboxylation)
- **CO₂** (from decarboxylation reactions)
- **H₂** (from formate lyase or hydrogenase under anaerobic conditions)

At turn 5, glucose is gone. I must shift to fermentation of:

Given I showed **zero growth at turn 4**, I need to reassess. The most likely explanation: I ran out of readily fermentable carbohydrate (glucose) and may have miscalculated or attempted unsustainable growth. Now I must rely on lactate, arabinose, and amino acid fermentation.

## 4. Growth calculation

**Carbon budget:**
- Uptake: L-lactate (10 mmol/gDW/h) → 2C per lactate = 20 C
- Uptake: L-arabinose (5 mmol/gDW/h) → 5C per arabinose = 25 C
- Uptake: L-arginine (2 mmol/gDW/h) → 6C per arg = 12 C
- **Total C in: ~57 mmol C/gDW/h**

**Carbon costs:**
- Growth at rate μ (1/h): 38 mmol C per g biomass produced = 38μ mmol C/gDW/h
- Fermentation products (acetate, butyrate, formate, CO₂): these are carbon skeletons released

For sustainable growth without accumulating errors, I estimate:
- μ ≈ **0.35 /h** (moderate growth, given depleted carbohydrate but available lactate + arabinose + amino acids)

**Nitrogen budget:**
- Uptake: L-arginine (2 mmol/gDW/h) → 4 N per arg = 8 N
- Uptake: L-serine (1 mmol/gDW/h) → 1 N per ser = 1 N
- Uptake: Ammonium (1 mmol/gDW/h) → 1 N per NH₄ = 1 N
- **Total N in: ~10 mmol N/gDW/h**
- Growth cost: 10 mmol N per g biomass = 10μ = 3.5 mmol N/gDW/h at μ=0.35
- **Sufficient nitrogen available.**

**Carbon audit at μ = 0.35 /h:**
- C in: 57 mmol C/gDW/h
- C for growth: 38 × 0.35 = 13.3 mmol C/gDW/h
- C for fermentation products: 57 − 13.3 = 43.7 mmol C/gDW/h

I will release this as acetate, butyrate, formate, and CO₂ in proportions consistent with mixed-acid fermentation of lactate and arabinose.
