## Fermentation strategy

- Turns 1-2: Glucose fermentation → acetate, propionate, formate (high growth 0.65 /h)
- Turn 3: Lactate utilization when glucose depleted
- Turn 4: Amino acid fermentation (proline, alanine), zero growth

**Current situation (turn 5):** Glucose is exhausted. I must shift to alternative carbon/nitrogen sources. The amino acid pool is still substantial and offers both carbon and nitrogen.
