# Metabolic decision for Eubacterium rectale ATCC 33656 at t=0

## Analysis of transporters and available substrates

Eubacterium rectale is a saccharolytic and proteolytic Gram-positive anaerobe from the human gut microbiota. My genome encodes:
- Carbohydrate transporters: PTS systems and permeases for glucose, arabinose, lactate, and other sugars
- Amino acid transporters: broad specificity for proteinogenic amino acids
- I can ferment sugars to short-chain fatty acids and lactate

**Available carbon sources I can use:**
- D-glucose (24.98 mM): excellent substrate, high affinity
- L-lactate (28.31 mM): can ferment lactate
- L-arabinose (21.31 mM): pentose, can metabolize
- Maltose (4.382 mM): disaccharide, can ferment
- Amino acids: extensive pool for catabolism

**Nitrogen sources:**
- Ammonium (9.347 mM): primary nitrogen source
- Amino acids: can be deaminated and used for both carbon and nitrogen

## Fermentation strategy

As an obligate anaerobe, I ferment carbohydrates to short-chain fatty acids (acetate, butyrate, propionate) and lactate. Given the abundance of glucose and lactate, I will pursue mixed-acid fermentation. My typical products are acetate, butyrate, and propionate, with some formate and H₂.

**Growth requirement:** 33 mmol C per gram dry weight produced
