## Fermentation strategy
My known fermentation products are **butyrate** and **acetate** (with minor formate and CO₂). I do not produce lactate; that was consumed from the environment. My core metabolism converts sugars and amino acids to acetyl-CoA, then to butyrate via the butyryl-CoA pathway.

## Optimal substrate choice
**L-arabinose** is abundant (21.31 mM) and a preferred fermentable sugar for me. I should prioritize this. I will also continue modest uptake of **L-arginine** for nitrogen and additional carbon, and consume remaining **D-glucose**.
